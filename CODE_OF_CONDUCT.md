# Code of conduct

This project welcomes contributors who help make worker information safer, clearer, and more useful.

## Expected conduct

- Treat workers, contributors, and maintainers with dignity.
- Critique claims, code, and decisions without harassment or personal attacks.
- Respect privacy. Never pressure someone to disclose an employer, workplace conflict, organizing status, legal status, or identity.
- Keep disagreements evidence-based and relevant to the project.
- Accept correction, disclose conflicts of interest, and distinguish personal experience from verified fact.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, stalking, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate exposure of sensitive worker information, and attempts to use project spaces for employer surveillance or retaliation are not permitted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the process in `SECURITY.md` when public discussion would expose someone. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or refer credible threats to the appropriate service operator or authority.

Enforcement decisions should protect affected people, preserve relevant evidence, minimize unnecessary disclosure, and avoid retaliation against good-faith reporters.
